Carmen Hove, PhD, NASM-CPT

Hi, I'm Carmen, founder and owner of Reda (RAY-duh) Fitness. Reda is the Swedish word for "clarity." When it comes to your fitness journey, I keep things straightforward. No fads, no hype, no upselling you on supplements. Most of the hard things in life operate according to simple principles—you just need to know how and where to begin and then commit to the process. It doesn't matter where you begin; all that matters is that you're ready to move forward!

I have a BS in Applied Human Biology, a PhD in Biological Anthropology and am a Certified Personal Trainer through the National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM). My programs focus on strength, stability, endurance, balance, and agility using multiple modalities (free weights, body weight, etc.) and are further tailored to client needs and goals. Contact me today to schedule an initial session!

Services

In-Person Training

Individual or small-group sessions based in Kingston, WA for clients living in Kitsap County (Bainbridge Island WA, Poulsbo WA, Kingston WA, Suquamish WA, Silverdale WA). Offered as 60 minute sessions.

Online Training

Structured goal-specific plans for remote clients who prefer to train virtually. I use Quickcoach to build and send plans. No complicated apps or annoying software. Offered as monthly plans (mesocycles).

Pricing

In-Person Training

My hourly fee for a single-client session is $100. For a 60 minute group session, my fee is $90 per person (10% discount for each person).

Your first session has zero strings attached. If you decide to continue training with me, I operate using a monthly subscription model where you can choose between 1-12 sessions per month. This allows me to block off consistent training times for each client without shouldering the financial risk of late cancellations or no-shows. You can cancel your subscription at any time without penalty, at which point you'll simply forfeit your dedicated spot on my schedule. You can pick back up later, as my schedule allows!
